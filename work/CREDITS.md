- All other geometry (rooms, counters, signs, booths, stalls, stage, desks) is procedural, built with `source_scripts/work_assets.py`.
- `work_atlas.jpg` (POS signs, naira notes, menus, posters, chalkboard, screens, signboards) is drawn procedurally in `prep_work_tex.py` using Google Fonts (OFL): Montserrat, Oswald, Anton, Caveat.
