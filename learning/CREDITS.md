# Lagos Life · learning kit credits (Step 08, tag v7)

All third-party source assets are CC0 (public domain) from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com). They were reduced, re-materialled, combined and meshopt-compressed by `source_scripts/` (Blender kit pipeline: `lk_build_all.sh` → `learning_assets.py` → `finalize_lk.mjs` → compact). Everything else (lecture-hall shell, seating tiers and stage, bench desks, lectern, whiteboard, projector screen and AV trolley, noticeboard, sketchbook easel) is original work for Lagos Life.
