# Lagos Life · housing kit credits

All third-party source assets are CC0 (public domain) from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com). They were reduced, re-materialled, combined and meshopt-compressed by `source_scripts/` (Blender kit pipeline). Everything else (walls, doors, louvre windows, gate, generators' canopy, mattresses, beds, wardrobes, fridge, adire/aso-oke/print textures, etc.) is original work for Lagos Life.
