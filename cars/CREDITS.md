# Lagos Life · car kit credits (Step 10, tag v8)

All three vehicles (`car_sedan`, `car_suv`, `car_bike`) are original procedural models built for Lagos Life by `source_scripts/car_assets.py` (Blender 4.2, then meshopt-compacted). No third-party assets; released CC0 with the rest of this repo's original work. No taxi parts, liveries or brand marks. The `car_paint` material is near-white so the game tints it per model.
